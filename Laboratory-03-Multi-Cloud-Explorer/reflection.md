# Mission Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, AWS impressed me the most because of its broad range of services and global infrastructure. I found that AWS can support many different workloads, from simple applications to large enterprise systems.
Its flexibility makes it useful for organizations with different technical and business requirements.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform have many similarities. All three provide cloud computing services for computing, storage, networking, databases, identity, and other technologies. 
They also allow organizations to use scalable infrastructure without needing to maintain physical hardware themselves.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection because different organizations have different priorities.
For example, the university in this mission already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory, making Azure a suitable choice because of its Microsoft integration.
Meanwhile, an AI research company may benefit more from GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities, while a global e-commerce company needs strong availability and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform.
I learned to examine the client's budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability needs, and business goals before making a recommendation. This helped me understand how technical decisions should support business needs.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved after completing this mission.
I added organized documentation comparing AWS, Azure, and GCP, created recommendations for different clients, and documented Linux server information from KillerCoda.
The portfolio now demonstrates not only my technical knowledge but also my ability to research, compare solutions, make decisions, and present technical information clearly.
